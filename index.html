Oldest Surviving

We find the real one.

Oldest Surviving is a YouTube channel about the history of everyday things, words, places and habits. Every video shows the oldest surviving or first documented example of something ordinary, from a real museum or archive record, and tells its hidden story. Sources are cited in every description.

Researched from museum and archive records with AI tools; every script fact-checked, edited and approved by a person. Narrated with an AI voice; AI reconstructions are labelled on screen.

Oldest Surviving Uploader

This site is also the home of Oldest Surviving Uploader, a private, single-user tool that publishes the channel's own finished videos. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up: its only user is the channel owner, signed in with the channel's own Google account.

What the tool does, only on the Oldest Surviving channel:

  • Uploads finished, owner-approved videos as private with a scheduled publish time (YouTube Data API videos.insert).
  • Sets custom thumbnails on those videos (thumbnails.set).
  • Later: reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics to plan future topics (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

It never posts comments, never edits or deletes anything, and never acts on any other channel. It uses YouTube API Services; see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
