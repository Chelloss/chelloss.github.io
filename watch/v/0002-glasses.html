Eyeglasses Were Invented Around 1286. So Who Invented Them?

Nobody wrote down who made the first eyeglasses. A friar's sermon in 1306 dates them to about 1286, and a fresco in Treviso from 1352 is the oldest painting of anyone wearing them.

Watch on YouTube Subscribe

The story

Around 1286, someone in Italy made the first eyeglasses.2,3 And nobody wrote down his name.1,3 How do we know the date? In 1306, a friar preached in Florence:1,2

"It is not yet twenty years since this art was found. I saw the man who first made them."1,3 This is the oldest painting of anyone wearing glasses: Treviso, 1352.5,6 Look closely. Two lenses riveted together, simply pinched onto the nose.5,6

The joke? The cardinal in the painting died in the 1260s. He never wore glasses.2,6 Later, Florence claimed a local man, Salvino degli Armati, as the inventor.4,7 Historians call it a fraud, built in the 1600s on a faked gravestone text.4,7

The real inventor kept his secret. So every pair you own goes back to a stranger in Italy...1,3
