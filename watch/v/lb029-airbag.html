Your Steering Wheel Hides an Explosive. So Why Isn't the Airbag Like Hitting a Wall?

A crash sensor fires a charge in the steering wheel that fills the airbag with nitrogen in a few hundredths of a second. Vent holes then let the gas out as your head presses in, so the bag goes soft and is nearly empty by the time the car stops.
