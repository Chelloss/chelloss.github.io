Privacy

This website shows the Oldest Surviving videos with subtitles. It has no sign-in, no ads and no comments, and the site itself sets no cookies.

This site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; Cloudflare receives the page address, your browser type and your approximate country, and our counts never identify a person.

Your browser remembers the language you pick and your game progress, in its own local storage on your device. It never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

The videos play in YouTube's player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing loads from YouTube until you tap play. When you tap play, YouTube's player loads from Google's servers, and Google may store data in your browser; Google's privacy policy applies. Links to YouTube open YouTube.

Points, levels, badges and passport stamps come from your answers on this site. They are never given for watching videos, likes or subscribes. Without an account they stay only in your browser.

Anonymous totals

To see which stories and questions people like, the site adds +1 to daily totals, for example "a video was played" or "a question was answered right". These totals have no visitor id and no cookie, and nothing is stored on your device for them. They are sent to our database at Google Cloud in the European Union (Belgium), and only the site owner can read them.

Optional account (Sign in with Google)

You can sign in with Google to keep your game progress on every device. This is optional; the site works fully without it. Nothing loads from Google until you tap "Sign in with Google".

  • What we store: one record with your game progress (points, answers to the quick questions, badges, passport stamps and the date of the last change), linked to a random account number. We do not store your name, email or photo in it.
  • Sign-in: handled by Google's Firebase Authentication, which keeps your Google account's id, email and name so that you can sign in again. Firebase privacy and security.
  • Where: Google Cloud, in the European Union (Belgium).
  • Why: to give you the feature you asked for (your progress on every device). We never sell it, never use it for ads and never share it.
  • Your control: "Delete my account" on the Passport page deletes your progress and your sign-in account at once. You can also email us to ask what we hold or to delete it.
  • Age: the account is for people aged 14 and over, or younger with a parent's or guardian's OK.

The Subscribe buttons load YouTube's own button only when you tap them; then Google's privacy policy applies. The privacy policy of the Oldest Surviving YouTube tools is here. Contact: oldestsurviving@gmail.com.
